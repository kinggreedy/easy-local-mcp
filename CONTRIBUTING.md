# Contributing

## Contribution Philosophy

**AI-assisted and autonomous contributions are expected and welcome. Quality standards remain the same.**

This is my first experiment with this contribution model. I may get things wrong, and the policy will evolve.

- **Code quality:** Contributions are judged by their actual quality, not how they were produced. Substantial review before opening a PR is expected, whether performed by humans, AI agents, or both. Obvious code smells and low-quality contributions may be closed without detailed review.
- **Review process:** We work extensively with AI agents to review PRs, exercising human judgment and performing manual reviews where appropriate. Final decisions remain ours.
- **Contributor responsibility:** AI-assisted review discussions are welcome. However, when we explicitly request a human response to substantive concerns, contributors must answer themselves.
- **Review capacity:** Excessive, low-effort, or unresponsive submissions may be paused or closed.
- **Project alignment:** Even well-implemented contributions may be rejected if they conflict with our design philosophy or threat model. If your vision fundamentally differs from ours, we encourage you to fork the project and pursue your own direction. That's the beauty of open source.

**AI-friendly does not mean quality-blind.** Contributors should not expect us to repair or validate carelessly generated code.
